Ant Colony Activity, Brood Movement, and a Brief Vehicle-Like Visual Experience
Overview

During yard work on October 16, 2026, I observed unusual activity among ants in the yard, including ants near rocks in a worm bin carrying what appeared to be oversized, tan-colored pupae. I subsequently introduced finely crushed soda crackers near the ants and observed food collection. On returning outside at approximately 1:00 PM HST, I found the ants near the rocks still moving repeatedly around the same area. A separate location, where brood-like objects had previously been visible on a leaf near a fork, appeared to have been cleared.

During the same general observation session, I also experienced a brief vehicle-like visual impression after standing up from crouching over the ant colony.

This record documents the environmental observations, the sequence of events, and the subjective visual experience without presuming a shared cause.

Chronological Record

Event 1 — Initial Ant Colony Observation

Time: Earlier in the day; exact time unknown
Context: Yard work

While doing yard work, I noticed an ant colony that appeared to be unusually active. The ants seemed to be moving frantically and did not appear to be attending to their eggs as I expected.

I crouched over the area, attempting to understand what was happening so I could potentially help or determine the cause of the activity.

Some ants appeared to be carrying larger, tan-colored objects that I initially interpreted as baby queens.

Event 2 — Tan-Colored Pupae

I observed what appeared to be tan-colored pupae at different apparent sizes or developmental stages.

The objects I identified as pupae appeared to be approximately twice the size of mature worker ants. Their heads and middle sections appeared wider and rounder than those of the mature ants. 

The ants not carrying pupae appeared to be in a craze running around with no direction. 

The ants carrying pupae appeared to not know how to reorient themselves and figure out what they beeded to do. They kept running back and forth in and around the rocks in the bin.

Working hypothesis: Some of these individuals may be developing queens.

Identification status: Unconfirmed. The species and developmental stages have not been established. Appearance leans toward the queen pupae.

Event 3 — Brief Vehicle-Like Visual Experience

Time: During the same general observation session; exact time unknown

Context: Transition from crouching to standing and walking toward the driveway

After crouching over the ant colony, I stood up and walked toward the driveway.

I briefly saw what appeared to be a blueish vehicle in front of the driveway, facing toward the dead end of the road. The wall containing our mailbox obscured the front of the vehicle, while I saw the rear very quickly.

The experience was unusual because I seemed to perceive the entire vehicle mentally for a split second, even though the rear was the portion I saw with my eyes.

The vehicle seemed familiar, possibly resembling one that had traveled up the street previously, although I was uncertain about its identity.

When I checked the area, no vehicle was present.

Recorded characteristics:

Color: Blueish.

Orientation:  The vehicle-like image appeared oriented toward the dead end of Pōkā Place, in the same general direction as Waikīkī. The front was partially obscured by the mailbox wall; the rear was briefly visible.

Partial obstruction: Mailbox wall obscured the front.

Visible portion: Rear, very briefly.

Subjective experience: A brief visual impression accompanied by a sense of perceiving the whole vehicle.

Outcome: No vehicle was present when I checked.

Interpretive status: Cause undetermined. The record does not establish whether the experience arose from visual processing, memory, a transient perceptual event, or another cause.

Event 4 — Introduction of Soda Cracker Pieces

I broke soda crackers into very small pieces and placed them near the ants.

Afterward, many ants began carrying pieces of the crackers. The colony appeared to become calmer.

Direct observation: Ants transported pieces of the offered food.

Initial interpretation: Food collection may have redirected some of the ants' activity.

Uncertainty: The apparent calming may have coincided with a natural change in colony behavior. No controlled comparison was performed.

Event 5 — Return to the Observation Site

Timestamp: 2026-10-16T13:00:00-10:00
Local time: 1:00 PM HST

I returned outside to continue observing the ants.

A. Worm Bin and Rocks

The ants carrying the oversized pupae were still in the same general area around the rocks in the worm bin.

They continued moving back and forth and around and around in that area.

At this checkpoint, I noted that the repetitive movement persisted. Whether they were still carrying pupae at this precise moment was not separately confirmed.

B. Leaf Near the Fork

A previous photograph showed numerous egg-like or brood-like objects on a leaf near a fork.

On returning, I observed that the objects appeared to have been removed from the leaf.

Working hypothesis: The ants may have relocated the brood.

Uncertainty: The objects' identity and destination have not been confirmed.
