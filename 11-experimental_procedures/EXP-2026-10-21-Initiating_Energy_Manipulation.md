To investigate whether sustained mental imagery of wave-like energy patterns can improve perceived control, localization, and modulation of internal sensations over time.

Assume a comfortable seated or resting position and minimize external distractions for the next 30 minutes.

Form a mental image of a wave or concentrated mass of energy within the body or mind.

Visualize the energy originating from one location and gradually increasing in intensity.

Mentally guide the energy through one or more of the following movements while in a relaxed state:

Rocking back and forth.

Shifting from side to side.

Traveling along a defined path.

Rotating or rolling like a sphere.

Expanding and contracting in size.

Sustain the visualization while intermittently increasing or decreasing the perceived intensity, size, or movement of the wave.

Maintain awareness of any changes in bodily sensation, tension, temperature, pressure, activation, relaxation, or perceived energy distribution.

Repeat the exercise for a fixed duration or until a clear change in perception, control, or sensation is observed.
