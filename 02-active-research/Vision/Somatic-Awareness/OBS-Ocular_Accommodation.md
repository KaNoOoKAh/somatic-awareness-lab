# Observation Log

## Baseline

Contracting the eye with an effort level of around 2, and attempting to flatten or tighten the ciliary muscles. 

---

## Trial 1

Date:10/13/2026
Time:03:10p.m.

Condition:
- Relaxed state 
 - While attempting to contract the the ciliary body and muscles.

Duration:
10 minutes

Observed Sensations:
- Right eye started to feel discomfort near the top right side. 
- Control seemed to get directed towards the retina area of both eyes. 

Visual Effects:

-While looking at the beach across the street, approximately one mile away, the image gradually became blurrier the longer I attempted to maintain a contracted accommodative state.

-After placing my hand close to my face, my hand appeared very sharp and well focused.

-At the same time, the surrounding environment appeared comparatively blurred and out of focus. 

-The difference between the focus of my hand and the focus of distant objects was easy to observe. I should have attempted to take control of the different visuals instead of letting it happen randomly.

Physical Effects:

- A noticeable difference in bodily sensation was observed between the left and right eyes.
- Sensations associated with the left eye were less distinct and appeared more difficult to perceive consistently than sensations associated with the right eye.
- Increased awareness was present near the upper front region of the left eye.
- During the observation period, awareness of the right upper eyelid gradually increased.
- Toward the end of the observation, the entire upper right eyelid became more noticeable within conscious awareness.(I was able to feel the right eye-lid on top of the eye.)
- Both eye regions appeared easy to monitor. Specific control was hard to initiate.
  
Recovery Time:
3:21p.m. HST 

- Intentional near-focus effort was discontinued at 3:21 PM HST.
- Following discontinuation, feeling associated with the sustained accommodative effort gradually decreased.
- Attention was directed toward the inner parts of the eyes and not the surrounding ocular region.
- During inhalation and exhalation, awareness was maintained on sensations arising from the eyes.
- No physical pressure was intentionally applied to the eyes or surrounding tissues. The force was similiar to increasing circulation. Which I have done to the face and other body regions while making sure I was able to supply myself with oxygen instead of holding my breath. 

Notes: 

Tension in the recovery state was immediately lifted when giving my body directions and helping in the process.

Reference Note: 

College Physics by Hugh D.Young, Version: 9th Edition.

"When the ciliary muscle surrounding the lens contracts, the lens bulges and the radii curvature of its surfaces decreases thereby decreasing the focal length. For the normal eye, an object at infinity is sharply focused when the ciliary muscle is relaxed. With increasing tension, the focal length decreases to permit sharp imaging on the retina of closer objects. The process is called accommodation."
